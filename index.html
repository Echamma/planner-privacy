Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Planner collects nothing, transmits nothing, and stores everything on your own device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What the app stores

Everything you write — time blocks, tasks, notes, habits, log entries, goals and deadlines — is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. Android prevents other applications from reading it.

What leaves your device

Nothing.

Planner does not request internet access. It holds no network permission, so it is not technically capable of sending your information anywhere — to us or to anyone else. There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party component of any kind.

This is verifiable rather than promised: the app declares exactly three permissions, and none of them permit network access.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Notifications To show reminders for your time blocks and the daily summary
Run at startup To restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts
Alarms & reminders So a reminder set for a particular time arrives at that time

None of these involve collecting or transmitting information.

Backups

If you have Android's system backup switched on, your phone's operating system may include this app's data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under their own privacy policy. That is a function of Android rather than of this app, and you can exclude Planner in your device's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored. You can also clear it at any time from Android's app settings, under Storage.

Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete at our end and no request you need to make of us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
