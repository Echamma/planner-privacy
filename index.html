Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Planner has no account, no cloud service and no analytics. Everything you write is stored on your own device. The app can optionally be paired with a Planner server that you run on your own computer — nothing is ever sent to us, or to any third party.

What the app stores

Everything you write — time blocks, tasks, notes, habits, log entries, goals and deadlines — is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. Android prevents other applications from reading it.

If you pair the app with your own computer, it also stores that computer's address and a token the computer issued, so it does not have to be paired again each time.

What leaves your device

Nothing, until you pair.

There is no service for us to send anything to. The only address the app will ever contact is one you type in yourself during pairing, pointing at a Planner server running on a machine you control, on your own network. There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party component of any kind.

If you never pair, nothing leaves your phone.

When you do pair

Pairing sends your phone's model name — “Pixel 8”, for example — to that computer, so you can tell your devices apart in its list. The computer sends back a token, which the app stores to identify itself afterwards.

Your planner content travels only between your phone and the computer you paired with. It does not pass through us or through anyone else, because there is nothing in between.

This traffic is not encrypted. It is plain HTTP across your own local network, a deliberate trade for a connection that never leaves your home — but it does mean anyone already on that network could read it. Do not pair over Wi-Fi you do not trust.

Permissions

Local network Newer Android versions ask for this separately, so the app can find and reach that computer on your own network
Nearby devices For an optional direct Bluetooth link to your own computer. It is used only if you choose that connection
Notifications To show reminders for your time blocks and the daily summary
Run at startup To restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts
Alarms & reminders So a reminder set for a particular time arrives at that time

Only the first three involve a connection at all, and only towards a machine you nominated yourself.

Backups

If you have Android's system backup switched on, your phone's operating system may include this app's data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under their own privacy policy. That is a function of Android rather than of this app, and you can exclude Planner in your device's backup settings.

The pairing token is deliberately left out of backups. It identifies one phone, so restoring it onto a different one would leave two devices sharing a single credential. Pair again instead.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored. You can also clear it at any time from Android's app settings, under Storage.

If you paired with your own computer, the copy held there is your own file, on your own machine, for you to delete as you see fit.

Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete at our end and no request you need to make of us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
